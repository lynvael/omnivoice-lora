Both CIs exclude 0 — the gap is real, not noise. **Power caveat (measured, not
assumed):** the per-phrase correlation between the two baselines is r ≈ −0.10,
so pairing does NOT reduce variance. The marginal SE of a single variant's SIM
is ≈ 0.016–0.019, while the paired SE is ≈ 0.026 (0.0263 sim_best / 0.0284

not detectable at n=20, and two finalists within that band require a listening
check (or the documented 60-phrase expansion).

## Flagged clips (sanity gates)

- `base_native p17: too_fast` — 7 words in 0.73 s = 9.59 words/s. Over the 612
  real Mita clips words/s is mean 2.16, median 2.12, p95 3.39, max 4.17, and
  0.00% exceed 5.0 — so > 5.0 wps is a truncation, not speech. This is the
  generation-stability failure behind the p17 WER error.
- No other flags. All 40 clips: RMS 0.067-0.123 (gate: < 1e-4 = silence),
  duration 0.73-5.24 s (gates: < 0.3 s / > 20 s).
- **Gate change:** the old clipping gate (peak > 0.999) was removed — upstream
  post-processing peak-normalises to exactly 0.5 (every rendered clip peaks at
  0.50), so the gate could never fire. No low-rate gate was added: 16.7% of
  real Mita clips are < 1.5 words/s because of silences.

## Metric definitions

- **WER** = (S + D + I) / (total reference words), pooled over all 20 phrases
  (not the mean of per-phrase WERs). S/D/I come from a word-level Levenshtein
  DP with backtrace. ASR: faster-whisper large-v3, `language="ru"`, beam 5,
  temperature 0, no VAD, no previous-text conditioning, CUDA fp16.
  Normalisation (both sides): Unicode NFKC, lowercase, ё→е, every character
  that is not a Cyrillic/Latin letter or digit → space, whitespace collapse,
  then every whitespace-separated pure-integer token (`^\d+$`) is verbalised
  with `num2words(int(tok), lang="ru")` (v0.5.14) and whitespace re-collapsed.
  Known limitation: num2words handles cardinal integers only — ordinals
  ("первый"), decimals, and compound units are not verbalised; the frozen set
  contains no such tokens, so this does not affect these numbers.
- **sim_centroid_*** = cosine between the clip embedding and the L2-normalised
  mean of the 60 reference embeddings; ceiling = mean cosine of each reference
  to that centroid (0.612105).
- **sim_best_*** (primary) = max cosine between the clip embedding and the 60
  individual reference embeddings; ceiling = leave-one-out max (each reference
  vs the other 59, mean 0.606127). All embeddings L2-normalised; cosine = dot.
- **WER verdict** (veto): `pass` iff pooled WER <= 3% AND no single phrase
  WER > 50%; otherwise `fail`. A `fail` disqualifies a candidate checkpoint;
  it does not rank candidates.
- **n=20 power caveat:** the measured per-phrase correlation between the two
  baselines is r ≈ −0.10, so pairing does not reduce variance: marginal SE ≈
  0.016–0.019, paired SE ≈ 0.026, practical resolution ≈ 0.05 on a delta. An
  improvement of 0.03 is not detectable at n=20; two finalists within that
  band require a listening check (or the documented 60-phrase expansion).
